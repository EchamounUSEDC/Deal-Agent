# Nug's Tour of Central City, Colorado — Rough Draft Script

**Open:** ~6–8s of poker cards & chips falling (the WYLDE intro), no narration.

**Scene 1 — Nug intro** (over Nug close-up / fades in):
> "Well, howdy there, partners! I'm Nug — a little gold nugget with a big ol' story to tell. Come along with me to one of Colorado's most famous mountain towns!"

**Scene 2 — Snowy aerial of Central City:**
> "This here is Central City, Colorado, tucked high in the Rocky Mountains at over eight thousand five hundred feet. Folks once called it 'The Richest Square Mile on Earth!'"

**Scene 3 — Historic 1800s mining photo (black & white):**
> "Way back in 1859, a prospector named John Gregory struck gold right here in Gregory Gulch. Word spread like wildfire, and thousands of miners rushed in, building this whole town almost overnight!"

**Scene 4 — Bonanza Casino / Main Street:**
> "Today, Central City's historic Main Street is alive again! You can try your luck at the casinos, catch a show at the grand old Opera House, and stroll past beautiful buildings from the gold rush days."

**Scene 5 — Outro (Nug waves):**
> "So come on up and visit Central City — where Colorado's golden history still shines bright! This is Nug, wishin' you good luck, and happy trails!"

Total narration ≈ 60–75 seconds. Nug "walks" across each scene as he talks; his lines also appear as captions.
